Site Man privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Site Man is an Android app for keeping site records, forms, drawings, photos and reminders on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version: there is no account and no Site Man server. What you create stays on your phone, apart from the optional weather look-ups and the dictation described below.

Who we are

Site Man is developed and published by the developer named on its Google Play listing (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at isaacwong.sse@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • projects, site records, forms, record templates and their answers;
  • drawings, imported PDFs, photos, scans and captions;
  • reminders and schedule entries, and the times they are set to alert;
  • app settings, including the display language, text size and, if you enter one, the name attached to templates and cards you share;
  • your choice of app lock.

This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it, and we have no server that holds a copy.

What leaves your phone

The app has no account system and does not upload your records. Three optional features use the network or the phone's own services:

Weather

When you open the weather page, the app sends requests to:

  • the Hong Kong Observatory open-data API (data.weather.gov.hk) and radar images (www.hko.gov.hk);
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) for the hourly forecast.

These requests carry fixed Hong Kong coordinates (22.30° N, 114.17° E). The app does not request the location permission and never reads your device's location. As with any internet request, the service receives your IP address and may log it under its own privacy policy: hko.gov.hk privacy policy and open-meteo.com terms. Weather is optional; the rest of the app works offline.

Dictation

When you tap the microphone, the app uses Android's speech recognition service. The microphone is used only while the dictation sheet is open, and the app does not keep a recording afterwards.

  • On phones with on-device speech recognition, the app asks Android to recognise speech offline, and the audio is processed on the phone.
  • On other phones, the system speech service may send the audio to its provider (for example Google) for processing. That is controlled by the phone and its speech provider, not by Site Man; check the provider's privacy policy and your phone's speech settings.

Site Man itself does not upload audio. Anything the recogniser returns is placed in the field you are filling in, and is saved only if you save the form.

Translation and AI

The translation and “Create with AI” features use Android's on-device AI (Gemini Nano through ML Kit GenAI). They are offered only when the phone has the on-device model installed. The text is processed on the device; the app does not send it to us or to any server. If the phone does not support them, the buttons are disabled and the app says why.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. Sharing uses Android's own share sheet: the app hands the item you picked to the app you pick. We never receive a copy.

Camera and scanning

The camera is used only while the scanner or a photo attachment is open. Camera images are processed on the device by the scanning feature.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK that sends data off the device.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, photos library or location.

Storage, backups and deletion

Data lives in the app's private storage. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app (allowBackup=false and explicit extraction rules), so records, photos and drawings are not copied into a Google backup or onto a new phone.

You can delete individual items inside the app. Uninstalling Site Man removes its private storage, and with it everything the app saved. Because we hold no copy, deletion is permanent.

Security

The app relies on Android's app sandbox to keep its files away from other apps. If you turn on the optional app lock, unlocking uses Android's biometric prompt (fingerprint, face or the device PIN/pattern/password). Biometric data is handled by Android and is never available to Site Man.

Children

Site Man is a tool for site work and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app's data handling: isaacwong.sse@gmail.com.

If you are in Hong Kong and are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data: pcpd.org.hk.

Site Man 私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 9 日

Site Man 是一個 Android 應用程式，讓你把工地記錄、表單、圖則、相片與提醒保存在手機上。本政策說明這個應用程式如何處理資料。

簡而言之：本程式沒有帳戶，也沒有 Site Man 伺服器。你建立的內容都留在你的手機，只有下文所述的天氣查詢與聽寫會涉及外部服務。

我們是誰

Site Man 由 Google Play 商店頁面上列明的開發者開發及發佈（下稱「我們」）。聯絡電郵：isaacwong.sse@gmail.com。

留在你手機上的資料

本程式把以下資料儲存在裝置上的私人空間：

  • 項目、工地記錄、表單、記錄模板及其答案；
  • 圖則、匯入的 PDF、相片、掃描影像與說明；
  • 提醒與日程，以及你設定的提示時間；
  • 應用程式設定，包括顯示語言、文字大小，以及（如你填寫）附加於你分享的模板與卡片上的姓名；
  • 你的應用程式鎖設定。

這些資料不會傳送給我們。我們看不到，也沒有伺服器保存副本。

離開你手機的資料

本程式沒有帳戶系統，也不會上傳你的記錄。以下三項可選功能會使用網絡或手機本身的服務：

天氣

當你開啟天氣頁時，本程式會向以下服務發出請求：

  • 香港天文台開放數據 API（data.weather.gov.hk）及雷達圖像（www.hko.gov.hk）；
  • Open-Meteo（api.open-meteo.com），用於逐小時預報。

這些請求使用固定的香港座標（北緯 22.30 度、東經 114.17 度）。本程式不請求定位權限，也從不讀取你的裝置位置。與任何互聯網請求一樣，服務供應商會收到你的 IP 位址，並可能按其私隱政策記錄：香港天文台私隱政策及 Open-Meteo 條款。天氣功能是可選的；程式其餘部分可離線使用。

聽寫

當你按下麥克風，本程式會使用 Android 的語音辨識服務。麥克風只在聽寫畫面開啟時使用，程式事後不會保留錄音。

  • 在具備裝置端語音辨識的手機上，本程式會要求 Android 離線辨識，音訊在手機上處理。
  • 在其他手機上，系統語音服務可能把音訊傳送給其供應商（例如 Google）處理。這由手機及其語音服務供應商控制，並非由 Site Man 控制；請參閱該供應商的私隱政策及手機的語音設定。

Site Man 本身不會上傳音訊。辨識結果只會填入你正在編輯的欄位，並只在你儲存表單時才會保存。

翻譯與 AI

翻譯及「用 AI 建立」功能使用 Android 的裝置端 AI（透過 ML Kit GenAI 使用 Gemini Nano）。只有在手機已安裝裝置端模型時才會提供。文字在裝置上處理；本程式不會把文字傳送給我們或任何伺服器。如手機不支援，按鈕會停用，程式亦會說明原因。

分享

除非你選擇分享，否則不會分享任何內容。分享經 Android 內建的分享選單進行：本程式把你選取的項目交給你選取的應用程式。我們不會收到副本。

相機與掃描

相機只在掃描器或附加相片時使用。相機影像由掃描功能在裝置上處理。

我們不會做的事

  • 不設帳戶、註冊或登入。
  • 沒有廣告，也不使用廣告識別碼。
  • 沒有會把資料送出裝置的分析、當機回報或追蹤 SDK。
  • 不會出售或出租個人資料。
  • 不會讀取你的聯絡人、日曆、相片庫或位置。

你可以拒絕任何一項權限，程式其餘部分仍可運作。

儲存、備份與刪除

資料保存在應用程式的私人空間。本程式已關閉 Android 雲端備份與裝置間轉移（allowBackup=false 及明確的排除規則），因此記錄、相片與圖則不會被複製到 Google 備份或新手機。

你可以在程式內刪除個別項目。解除安裝 Site Man 會移除其私人空間，連同程式保存的一切資料。由於我們沒有副本，刪除後無法復原。

保安

本程式依賴 Android 的應用程式沙箱，把其檔案與其他應用程式隔離。如你開啟可選的應用程式鎖，解鎖會使用 Android 的生物辨識提示（指紋、人臉或裝置的 PIN／圖案／密碼）。生物辨識資料由 Android 處理，Site Man 永遠無法取得。

兒童

Site Man 是工地工作工具，並非以兒童為對象。我們不會在知情下收集兒童的資料。

政策更新

如本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁及頁首日期。重大變更亦會在本程式的版本說明中註明。

聯絡我們

如對本政策或本程式的資料處理有疑問：isaacwong.sse@gmail.com。

如你身處香港而對我們的回覆不滿意，可聯絡個人資料私隱專員公署：pcpd.org.hk。
